# Council composition and vacancies

The **Sangguniang Panlungsod** is the legislative body of **{GOVERNMENT_NAME}**. It passes the city's ordinances and resolutions and approves its budget. The Vice Mayor presides over the council.

According to the results of the May 2025 local elections, voters elected **10 city councilors** for the 2025–2028 term, who assumed office on June 30, 2025.

---

## Current situation

On **October 16, 2026**, news outlets reported that the Office of the Ombudsman ordered the dismissal from service of six members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, along with the Mayor and Vice Mayor. The six councilors named in the reports are:

- Franco Paolo Del Prado
- Warren Andrada
- Aurelio Agsalud
- Franklin Villanueva
- Jhan Hero Sumera
- Alfonso Miguel Del Prado

The reports say they were found liable for gross neglect of duty for refusing to assume the acting posts assigned to them by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG).

Councilors Blesildo F. Sumera and Rio Virgilio R. Esteves, who earlier took the acting posts, now lead the city as Mayor and Vice Mayor. Because of the dismissals, the council has **several vacant seats**.

The DILG has said it is studying how to fill them under the Local Government Code and the city charter. Reports say the remaining councilors may nominate qualified individuals for the vacancies, subject to legal procedures. The exact list of sitting councilors has not been published in the reports we reviewed, so this page does not list a full roster yet.

This page will be updated once the council's membership is confirmed from an official source.
