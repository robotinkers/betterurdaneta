# New Urdaneta leadership appeals for cooperation

**Reported October 21, 2026**

The Manila Times reported that Mayor Blesildo Sumera and Vice Mayor Rio Virgilio Esteves, both councilors before the Ombudsman's dismissal order took effect, were installed after the DILG directed them to take their oaths.

- Six council seats became vacant after the dismissal of six councilors.
- For quorum, the actual number of sitting councilors is the basis, and the vacancies are treated as permanent unless changed through lawful process.
- The DILG is studying how to fill the vacancies under the Local Government Code and the city charter. The remaining councilors may nominate qualified individuals, subject to legal procedures.
