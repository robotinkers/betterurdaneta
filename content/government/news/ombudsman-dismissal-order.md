# Ombudsman orders dismissal of Urdaneta mayor, vice mayor and six councilors

**Reported October 16, 2026**

News outlets reported that the Office of the Ombudsman ordered the dismissal from service of Mayor Julio "Rammy" F. Parayno III, Vice Mayor Jimmy D. Parayno and six members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod. The decision is dated **April 15, 2026**, and city officials said they received it on October 15, 2026.

- The Mayor and Vice Mayor were found liable for **grave misconduct** for continuing to perform official duties despite a 12-month suspension ordered by the Office of the President in January 2025.
- The six councilors were found liable for **gross neglect of duty** for refusing to assume acting posts assigned to them by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG). They are Franco Paolo San Juan Del Prado, Warren Dela Cruz Andrada, Aurelio L. Agsalud, Franklin V. Villanueva, Jhan Hero M. Sumera and Alfonso Miguel Manuel Del Prado.
- The penalties reported include perpetual disqualification from public office, cancellation of civil service eligibility and forfeiture of retirement benefits.
- The Philippine Daily Inquirer reported that several other charges against the Mayor and Vice Mayor were dismissed.
- The Inquirer reported that none of the officials had issued a statement at the time of publication.
