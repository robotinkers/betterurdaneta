# Two councilors assume the top Urdaneta posts

**Reported April 1, 2025**

The Daily Tribune reported that Councilors Rio Virgilio Esteves and Blesildo Sumera took the acting mayor and vice mayor posts on March 31, 2025, after the suspension of Mayor Julio Parayno III and Vice Mayor Jimmy Parayno was enforced. The DILG carried out the suspension order from the Office of the President on March 14, 2025, with support from Police Regional Office 1. The report said the suspended Mayor publicly contested the suspension and maintained his innocence.
